Two forms of the question

Form A – Discuss
What are the advantages and disadvantages of …?
→ Describe both sides.
Opinion NOT required
VS
Form B – Outweigh
Do the advantages of … outweigh the disadvantages?
→ Judge which side is bigger.
Opinion REQUIRED
outweigh = be more important than (nặng hơn, quan trọng hơn)

Essay structure

4 paragraphs · at least 250 words · 40 minutes (click to build)

Introduction
Paraphrase the question + your opinion
2–3 sentences
Body 1
Weaker side · “Admittedly, …” + 1–2 ideas
4–5 sentences
Body 2
Stronger side · “However, …” + 2 reasons with examples
5–6 sentences
Conclusion
“Although …, I believe …” · no new ideas
1–2 sentences
Body 1 = “Yes, there is this problem…” → Body 2 = “…but this is much more important.”

Intro and conclusion checklist

A good introduction…

  • paraphrases the question
  • gives a clear answer: which side is bigger
  • not just “This essay will discuss both sides.”

A good conclusion…

  • gives the same opinion as the introduction
  • summarises the main points
  • no new ideas
  • not “Both sides are equal.”

Model essay X-ray

1Nowadays, a growing number of employees do their jobs at home instead of in an office, especially since the Covid‑19 pandemic. 2Although this trend has some drawbacks, I believe its benefits are greater.

3Admittedly, working from home can cause some problems. 4Some workers feel lonely because they do not see their colleagues every day, and this can make them less motivated. 5This is especially true for young people who live alone and have few friends nearby. 6In addition, it is often difficult to separate work and home life. 7For example, many people check their emails late in the evening, so they never really relax and often feel tired the next day.

8However, these problems are less important than the advantages. 9Firstly, working from home saves a lot of time. 10Employees do not have to travel to the office, so they can spend an extra hour or two with their families, doing exercise or simply resting. 11For instance, a worker who used to spend two hours in traffic every day now has time to cook healthy meals. 12Secondly, it saves money for both workers and companies. 13Workers spend less on petrol, bus tickets and lunches, while companies can rent smaller offices and pay lower electricity bills. 14Moreover, many people find that they can concentrate better at home because it is quieter than a busy office, so they finish their tasks faster.

15In conclusion, although working from home can make some people feel lonely, I believe the time and money it saves are more important for both employees and employers.
